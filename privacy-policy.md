# Privacy Policy for VolumeBooster

Last updated: [Month DD, YYYY]

This Privacy Policy explains how VolumeBooster ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our volume booster and equalizer application available on Google Play. VolumeBooster processes audio primarily on your device, and we are committed to being transparent about the data the App accesses and how it is used.

## 1. Information We Access

VolumeBooster does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect or store your personal identity information (such as name, email address, or phone number) on our servers.

To provide its core functionality, the App requests the following device permissions:

- **Music and Audio / Storage** — used to read your on-device music library so tracks can be played and processed within the App.
- **Notifications** — used to show the media playback control notification.
- **Internet / Network Access** — used to load ads and send analytics and crash data.
- **Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)** — used to show the Edge Lighting overlay effect on your screen.
- **Foreground Service (Special Use)** — used to keep the Edge Lighting overlay feature running so it can respond to notifications and calls in real time.

Volume boosting, Bass Boost, Virtualizer, Equalizer, and Super Bass effects are applied locally on your device using Android's built-in audio effects framework. We do not access, record, upload, or otherwise transmit the content of your music or audio.

## 2. Advertising

VolumeBooster displays advertisements through Google AdMob (Banner, Interstitial, Native Advanced, and App Open ad formats). To deliver and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and process certain information automatically, which can include:

- Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
- IP address and approximate (coarse) location
- Device information (model, operating system version, language settings)
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, ad performance)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and is used to show relevant ads and prevent ad fraud. We do not have direct access to this data ourselves beyond aggregated, anonymized performance reports.

AdMob uses mediation to fill ad requests, including through **Meta Audience Network**. Mediation partners may independently collect and use information such as device identifiers and usage data, in accordance with their own privacy policies (see Section 5).

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings (Android Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization") or by adjusting your Google Account ad settings at [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com).

## 3. Analytics and Crash Reporting

We use **Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics** (Google) to understand how the App is used and to identify and fix crashes. These tools may collect information such as device model, operating system version, app usage events, and crash logs or stack traces. This data is processed in accordance with the [Firebase Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## 4. Information We Do Not Collect

- We do not require or collect account registration data.
- We do not collect, upload, or store the content of your music or audio on any server.
- We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
- We do not access your contacts, call logs, or messages.

## 6. Data Storage and Security

Your equalizer presets and effect settings (Bass Boost, Virtualizer, Super Bass, and band levels) are stored locally on your device. If you uninstall the App or clear its storage/cache, this locally saved data may be permanently deleted.

## 7. Children's Privacy

VolumeBooster is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising shown in the App is served via Google AdMob's standard settings, not a child-directed configuration.

## 8. Your Choices and Rights

Since the App does not maintain user accounts or server-side personal data, there is no account data for us to delete on request. You can manage or delete the data stored locally by the App at any time by:

- Resetting effect settings within the App
- Clearing the App's storage via Android Settings → Apps → VolumeBooster → Storage → Clear Storage
- Uninstalling the App

If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or California, you may have additional rights regarding data processed by our advertising, mediation, and analytics partners; please refer to the privacy policies linked in Section 5 to exercise those rights.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App's functionality or legal requirements. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
